## Honest weights and measures

Destination: `public/assets/kids/story-art/shuayb/the-call-and-deniers/p0/scene-v3.webp`

Prompt: Use case: illustration-story. A single landscape 16:9 children's storybook painting for an Islamic story about honesty in trading. Close intimate SIDE-ON shop interior scene in ancient Arabia, beautifully painted gouache and coloured-pencil texture. Dominant foreground at LEFT: a tall old wooden beam balance with two suspended bronze weighing pans, one contains grain, other proper stone weights. At right a huge open woven sack of golden wheat and a clay measure sit on a low counter. Background a dark plum shaded mud-brick niche with stacked small earthen jars, a narrow shaft of warm amber sunlight enters diagonally from a very small high opening at far right. Tight cropped objects, tactile fibres and warm ochre grain against deep purple shade. Entire frame devoted to an honest merchant's weighing tools, no town panorama, no landscape, no people, no religious figures, no text or lettering, no watermark. Visually rich, gentle and lovely for children. Balanced scale pans level, structurally plausible with two suspension cords.
