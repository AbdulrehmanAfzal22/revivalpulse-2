# Story artwork — current version 3

The current artwork changes and full visual review are in `docs/story-art-v3/`.
Open `docs/story-art-v3/index.html` to inspect all 69 stories, including chapter covers and paragraph pictures.

This revision changes the native drawing system across entire stories, keeps the 15 existing paintings, and adds three new, individually used paintings. Native illustrations and paintings are both used; the new set is not a complete replacement with AI-painted raster images.

Earlier `docs/story-art/` files describe the previous revision and are retained as historical notes. They do not establish the quality of the current version.

The original story text and the preceding voice/responsive fixes are preserved. Cloud-generated recordings of the whole story library are still pending the permission discussed earlier; this artwork update does not generate those recordings.
